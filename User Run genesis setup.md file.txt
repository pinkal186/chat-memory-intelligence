**What that actually does:**
- **G0 pre-flight** — checks the wiki/implementation-notes/codebase to confirm M1 isn't already built (it isn't — CURRENT.md currently says `next_action: run G0 existence pre-flight on M1`).
- **L1 BUILD** — up to 10 iterations. ## To STOP
- Just say "stop" or close the chat at any point — progress is safe because it's checkpointed **after every iteration**, not just at the end.
- It also stops itself automatically: 3 consecutive failed progress-gates (G2), the token/iteration budget is hit (50000 tokens / 10 iterations per milestone), or the same quality gate fails 3 times — in all cases it writes what it tried to CURRENT.md and surfaces to you instead of continuing blindly.

## To RESUME
- Open a fresh Agent chat (same or new session) and paste:
   > Read KICKOFF.md and resume — check CURRENT.md for where we left off.
- It reads CURRENT.md's `next_action`/`iteration`/`last_gate` fields and the milestone's checkpoint file, then continues instead of restarting the milestone from scratch.
